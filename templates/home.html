Eligibility Guidelines

Ensuring the safety of both our donors and recipients is our top priority.

Basic Requirements

  • Age: Must be between {{ config.min_age }} and {{ config.max_age }} years old.
  • Weight: Minimum weight of Donor, {{ config.min_weight|floatformat:0 }}kg required
  • Health: Must feel well and be in good general health on the day of donation.

Medical History

  • No major surgery in the last {{ config.surgery_deferral }} months.
  • Not currently taking antibiotics for active infections.
  • Must wait {{ config.deferral_period }} months after getting a tattoo or piercing.

Who Can Request?

  • Registered hospitals and Verified individuals
  • Emergency surgery requirements.
  • Chronic conditions (e.g., Thalassemia).
  • Accident or trauma victims.

Documentation Needed

  • Valid Medical Prescription from a registered doctor.
  • Patient ID and Hospital Details.
  • Blood Group Report (if available).
